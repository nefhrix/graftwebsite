We use personal data to provide and operate Graft. We may also use personal data to:

  • prevent fraud, misuse and unauthorised access;
  • understand how the website and Service are used;
  • improve Graft and develop new features;
  • send important service communications;
  • send product updates or marketing communications where permitted;
  • maintain business and accounting records; and
  • comply with legal, regulatory, tax and accounting obligations.

4. Document processing and Azure Document Intelligence

Graft uses Microsoft Azure Document Intelligence to analyse documents uploaded to the Service.

When you upload a receipt, invoice, statement or similar document, relevant document data may be transmitted to Microsoft Azure Document Intelligence so that information such as text, dates, suppliers, transaction amounts, invoice information and other document fields can be extracted.

The extracted information may then be returned to Graft and used to categorise, organise and report on your financial records.

Graft does not use automated document processing to make decisions about individuals that are intended to produce legal or similarly significant effects. You should review automatically extracted or categorised information before relying on it for tax, accounting or other important purposes.

5. Storage and Supabase

We use Supabase to provide backend infrastructure for Graft, including database, authentication and related application services.

Personal data associated with your account and information extracted from financial records may therefore be processed or stored using Supabase infrastructure.

6. Document storage and Cloudflare R2

We use Cloudflare R2 for object and document storage.

Financial documents that you upload to Graft, including receipts, invoices and statements, may be stored using Cloudflare R2. Access to stored documents is intended to be restricted to authorised users, systems and service providers that require access to operate the Service.

7. Payments and Stripe

We use Stripe to process subscription and other payments.

When you make a payment, Stripe may collect information such as your name, email address, billing information, payment method information and transaction details.

Payment-card information is processed by Stripe rather than being directly stored by Graft where Stripe's hosted payment infrastructure is used.

We may receive information from Stripe such as your payment status, subscription status, transaction identifiers and limited billing information so that we can administer your Graft subscription.

8. Google Analytics

Google Analytics may process information including pages viewed, approximate location derived from IP address, device and browser information, interactions with the website and similar usage information.

Where consent is required for non-essential analytics technologies, we will request that consent before using them. You can withdraw or change your cookie or analytics preferences through the cookie controls made available on our website, where applicable.

We do not retain every type of personal data for the same length of time. We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected and to satisfy applicable legal, accounting, tax, contractual and security requirements.

The seven-year period is a maximum policy period for relevant financial records and does not mean that the GDPR requires all personal data to be retained for seven years.

Account information will generally be retained for as long as your account remains active and for a reasonable period afterwards where required for legitimate business, security or legal purposes.

Documents that are no longer necessary may be deleted earlier, including where you validly request deletion and we have no overriding legal or contractual reason to retain them.

Analytics information is retained according to our configured analytics retention settings. Support communications and business correspondence may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to deal with enquiries, disputes, security matters and business record-keeping.

Backups may retain copies of information for a limited additional period before they are overwritten or securely deleted.
