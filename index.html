Our Story

Why we built Graft.

On a drive home from the airport, Adam and I were talking about how we saw our close friends and family who run their own businesses struggle with managing their receipts and invoices.

When I told him about the story of my girlfriend's mam's holiday being ruined because of a lost receipt, he told me his barber had a similar issue with paying hundreds for icloud storage of all his receipts, along with wasting time sorting through that dreaded box.

Coming from software engineeing, we knew we could build something useful to give our friends and family their time and peace of mind back.
